# 🍽️ OmniResto - Unified F&B Experience Platform

Welcome to **OmniResto**, a modern F&B platform designed exclusively for our single-brand restaurant chain. OmniResto bridges the gap between digital convenience and exceptional in-dining experiences across all of our physical locations.

---

## 🌟 Brand & Project Vision

As our restaurant chain expands, maintaining consistent food quality, operational excellence, and brand identity across every branch becomes our top priority. 

**OmniResto** was created to deliver a unified brand experience for our customers, whether they are ordering delivery from home, picking up on the go, or dining in at any of our branches. Powered by a centralized management ecosystem, OmniResto ensures every dish served reflects our core brand standards while adapting seamlessly to the local pace of each branch.

---

## 🔑 Core Pillars of the Platform

### 🛵 1. Unified Customer Experience
* **Seamless Online Delivery:** Customers enjoy a direct, branded ordering experience that automatically connects them to their nearest branch for optimal freshness and speed.
* **Smart Contactless Dine-In:** Guests seated at any branch can simply scan their table's QR code to explore the live menu, customize their orders, and pay effortlessly.
* **Chain-Wide Membership & Rewards:** A single, universal loyalty program where points earned at one branch can be redeemed across the entire chain.

### 🏢 2. Centralized Brand Control (HQ Management)
* **Master Menu Standardization:** Headquarters maintains full control over global menu offerings, promotional campaigns, and brand pricing.
* **Smart Order Routing:** Orders are automatically directed to the optimal branch location based on proximity and real-time kitchen capacity.
* **Cross-Branch Insights:** Real-time visibility into overall chain performance, popular dishes, and customer feedback.

### 🍳 3. Branch Autonomy & Operations
* **Kitchen Display Workflows:** Every branch operates with synchronized kitchen screens to ensure orders are prepared accurately and served hot.
* **Real-Time Local Stock Management:** Individual branches can instantly update item availability locally (e.g., if an ingredient runs out) without affecting other locations.
